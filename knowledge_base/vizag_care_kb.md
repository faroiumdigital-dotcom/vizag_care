- Dermatology (Ground Floor, Block C): Skin, hair and nail treatment including cosmetic dermatology. Treats: acne, skin allergy, eczema, psoriasis, hair fall, fungal infection. Services: Skin consultation, Laser treatment, Allergy testing, Hair treatments. Doctors: Dr. T. Harika.

- Gastroenterology (2nd Floor, Block A): Stomach, liver and digestive system care. Treats: acidity, stomach pain, jaundice, IBS, fatty liver, constipation, ulcers. Services: Endoscopy, Colonoscopy, Fibroscan, Liver clinic. Doctors: Dr. A. Prasad Varma.
